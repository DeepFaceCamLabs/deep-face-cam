# Responsible Use

Deep Face Cam can alter faces in images, videos, and live camera feeds. This
capability can be useful for consent-based creative work, testing, education,
and research. It can also be harmful when used deceptively.

## Allowed Uses

- Editing your own media or media where every identifiable person has consented.
- Internal testing, research, and development.
- Clearly labeled parody, satire, or creative work where the context is not
deceptive.
- Accessibility, localization, or production workflows that respect consent and
applicable law.

## Prohibited Uses

- Impersonation, fraud, scams, or bypassing identity checks.
- Non-consensual intimate imagery or sexualized content involving a real person.
- Harassment, stalking, blackmail, or reputational harm.
- Political, financial, legal, medical, or journalistic deception.
- Removing or weakening safety notices in distributed builds.

## Product Expectations

Public builds should make the following clear to users:

- The app runs locally.
- The user is responsible for consent and lawful use.
- Model files are downloaded only after an explicit prompt.
- Generated media should not be presented as authentic footage of someone.
